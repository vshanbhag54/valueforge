# Data-source policy

- Screener.in: accept user-authorised CSV exports; do not assume an official public API.
- Primary statements: company annual reports and quarterly results.
- Exchange disclosures: NSE/BSE public filings and shareholding announcements where access permits.
- Never automate login, bypass CAPTCHA/paywalls, or disguise scraping.
- Record original source URL, period end, publication/retrieval date, reporting basis and data freshness.
- Missing, stale or contradictory values must stay unknown and block confident conclusions.
- A clean NSE+BSE company master, SME/suspended-security treatment, duplicate listings, corporate actions and long-history normalization are still required for comprehensive coverage.
