# Research roles

1. Financial quality: profitability, returns, leverage, coverage and cash conversion.
2. Valuation: P/E and P/B screening; later normalized earnings, DCF and peer/history comparison.
3. Forensic accounting: pledging, auditor opinions, related parties, contingencies and cash conversion.
4. Industry analyst: cyclicality, competition, capital intensity and business economics.
5. Investment committee: reconcile disagreements and refuse positive conviction when evidence is missing.

Current roles are deterministic research modules. Later LLM agents must cite source facts, return unknown when evidence is absent and cannot override validation failures. Sector-specific rules are mandatory for banks, insurers and NBFCs. No trading or broker credentials.
